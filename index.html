01 · The Mob

Show up. Show the work. Leave.

Four to eight of us, one location, ten minutes. Live demos on screens, cards handed out, then we're gone. The footage does the rest.

How a drop works

Every drop runs to the same plan.

  1. 1

    Scout

    A busy spot where founders and creatives pass: Bree St, Kloof St, the Waterfront.

  2. 2

    Arrive

    The crew arrives from different directions and sets up screens and lights within a minute.

  3. 3

    Showcase

    Live websites, 3D web builds and dashboards on screen, with a QR code to the portfolio.

  4. 4

    Hand out

    Matte black NFC cards go to anyone who stops to watch, film or ask.

  5. 5

    Leave

    Screens off together, the crew splits up and walks off in different directions.
